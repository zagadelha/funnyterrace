How an engagement runs

Each stage ends with something you can decide on. You can stop after any of them and keep what was built.

  1. Discover

    2 weeks

    Interviews, a data review and a clear problem statement. You get a ranked list of AI opportunities and their expected value.

  2. Prove

    4 to 6 weeks

    A working proof of concept on your real data, with evaluation results, so the go or no-go decision rests on evidence.

  3. Build

    3 to 6 months

    Production engineering in short cycles: architecture, security, integrations and a release every two weeks.

  4. Run

    Ongoing

    Monitoring, model and prompt evaluation, cost tracking, and a handover your own team can own.
